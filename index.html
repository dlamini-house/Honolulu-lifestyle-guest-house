Understated Luxury

Refined Comfort in the Heart of Burgersfort

Honolulu Lifestyle Guesthouse represents a timeless blend of modern minimalist architecture and relaxed safari chic. Framed by a magnificent, private, fully secured garden, our estate offers a peaceful sanctuary where families, corporate professionals, and wilderness adventurers can experience high-end South African hospitality.

Every detail—from the crystal-clear swimming pool resting on beautiful checkerboard paving to our thatched lapa pavilion with massive stone pillars—communicates authenticity and elegance. Enjoy refreshing local wines by the pool, enjoy personalized service, and drift off to sleep in our meticulously styled, air-conditioned rooms.
